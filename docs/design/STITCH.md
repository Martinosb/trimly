## Design System Tokens Summary

- **Primary Brand Color:** `#ff385c` (Airbnb Rausch)
- **Background / Canvas:** `#ffffff` (Pure White) and `#f9f9f9` (Soft Surface)
- **Ink / Typography:** `#1a1c1c` (Headers), `#222222` (Display), `#5c3f41` (Subtext), `#6a6a6a` (Muted)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `1px solid #dddddd` / `#ebebeb`
- **Border Radii:** `8px` (`rounded-sm`), `14px` (`rounded-md` cards), `9999px` (`rounded-full` pills)
- **Button Standards:** 48px height, 14px/24px padding, pill-shaped primary actions with high contrast
- **Typography:** Inter (paired with tabular lining numbers for live slot matrices and GHS prices)
